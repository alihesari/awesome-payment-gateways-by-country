## Global Providers

- [Adyen](https://www.adyen.com/pricing) - Single platform for online, in-app and in-person card acquiring plus local payment methods. Fees: $0.13 fixed processing fee per transaction plus a payment method fee, for example Visa and Mastercard at interchange++ plus 0.60%, stated as indicative.
- [Airwallex](https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/pricing) - Payment acceptance, multi-currency accounts and payouts for businesses selling across borders. Fees: 2.80% + $0.30 domestic cards, 4.30% + $0.30 international cards.
- [Amazon Pay](https://pay.amazon.com/help/201212280) - Checkout button that lets shoppers pay with the payment methods saved in their Amazon account. Fees: 2.9% + $0.30 domestic, 3.9% + $0.30 for cards issued outside the US.
- [Apple Pay](https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/) - Wallet payment method for iOS, iPadOS, watchOS apps and Safari sites, added through your existing provider.
- [Authorize.net](https://www.authorize.net/sign-up/pricing.html) - Long-running gateway that connects a website to a merchant account, with an option to bundle processing. Fees: All-in-One $25 per month plus 2.9% + 30c, Gateway Only $25 per month plus 10c per transaction and a 10c daily batch fee.
- [Braintree](https://www.paypal.com/us/enterprise/paypal-braintree-fees) - Full-stack gateway and acquirer from PayPal supporting cards, wallets and local methods. Fees: 2.89% + 0.29 USD for cards and third-party digital wallets in the US.
- [Checkout.com](https://www.checkout.com/docs) - Direct acquiring, acceptance optimisation, card issuing and multi-currency accounts through one API.
- [dLocal](https://www.dlocal.com/) - Local pay-ins and payouts for emerging markets, connecting global merchants to regional cards, wallets and cash. Availability: more than 60 countries across Africa and the Middle East, Asia and Latin America.
- [GoCardless](https://gocardless.com/en-us/pricing) - Bank debit and open banking collection for recurring and invoice billing. Fees: Standard 0.5% + $0.05 domestic capped at $5, and 1.5% + $0.05 international.
- [Google Pay API](https://developers.google.com/pay/api) - Payment button for cards saved to a Google Account, added on top of your existing processor. Fees: no additional fee from Google, standard processing fees from your provider still apply.
- [Mollie](https://www.mollie.com/pricing) - European provider covering cards plus local methods such as iDEAL, Bancontact and SEPA. Fees: 1.80% + EUR 0.25 for EEA consumer cards, 3.25% + EUR 0.25 for non-EEA cards, iDEAL EUR 0.32.
- [Nuvei](https://docs.nuvei.com/documentation/home/) - End-to-end payment platform with hosted pages, SDKs, server-to-server APIs and alternative payment methods.
- [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/paypal-business-fees) - Wallet and checkout method with a large consumer base, usable alongside or instead of a card gateway. Fees: 3.49% plus a fixed fee for PayPal Checkout commercial transactions, $0.49 fixed in US dollars.
- [Rapyd](https://www.rapyd.net/products/pricing/) - Global acceptance, payouts, card issuing and multi-currency accounts with strong Latin American coverage. Fees: interchange 0.20% to 1.80% and scheme fees 0.02% to 0.65% per transaction, plus an acquirer fee quoted on request.
- [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/payments/our-fees) - Combined point of sale and online payments aimed at small and mid-sized sellers. Fees: online 3.3% + 30c on the Free plan and 2.9% + 30c on paid plans, in person 2.6% + 15c, keyed or card on file 3.5% + 15c.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/global) - Developer-focused payments platform covering cards, wallets, local methods, billing and marketplaces, with a country-by-country availability page.
- [SumUp](https://developer.sumup.com/) - Card readers and online payments for small merchants, with APIs and SDKs for online acceptance.
- [Wise Platform](https://wise.com/platform) - Cross-border payment and multi-currency account infrastructure for banks and businesses. Availability: 160+ countries and 40+ currencies.
- [Worldpay](https://www.worldpay.com/en) - Large global acquirer covering online and in-store acceptance, fraud tools, routing and payouts.

- [Freemius](https://freemius.com/pricing/) - Selling and licensing platform built around WordPress plugins and themes. Fees: 4.7% plus gateway fees, plus 2.3% for WordPress products, dropping on a tiered revenue share at higher volume.
- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/pricing) - Storefront and checkout for creators selling digital products, with sales tax collection and remittance. Fees: 10% + $0.50 per transaction for direct sales, 30% for sales through the Discover marketplace.
- [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) - Store and billing for digital products and SaaS, including global VAT and sales tax. Fees: 5% + 50c per transaction.
- [Nexway](https://www.nexway.com/) - Merchant of record model for global digital commerce, covering payments, tax and compliance.
- [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing) - Billing built for SaaS and apps, covering checkout, subscriptions, tax and compliance. Fees: 5% + 50c per Checkout transaction on pay as you go.
- [PayPro Global](https://payproglobal.com/pricing/) - Merchant of record for software and SaaS vendors, including global sales tax, VAT and invoice management.
- [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/fees) - Developer-oriented merchant of record for digital products, subscriptions and usage billing. Fees: 5% + 50c on the free plan, down to 3.4% + 30c on higher paid tiers, plus 1.5% for non-US cards.
